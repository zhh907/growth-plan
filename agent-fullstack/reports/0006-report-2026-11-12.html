你今天亲自问的词。/ˈruːbrɪk/,来自拉丁语 ruber(红色)——中世纪修道士用红墨水写章节指引,红字后来成了"照着执行的标准"。

① RUBRIC
The word you asked about yourself. /ˈruːbrɪk/, from Latin ruber (red) — medieval monks wrote chapter instructions in red ink, and the red letters became "the standard you follow".
Frequent pairs: grading rubric; tighten the rubric — your most-repeated action today. Same root family: ruby, rubella ("little red" disease).
② fan-out / fan-in — 扇出 / 扇入
下午代码的主角。fan-out = 任务从一点散出去给多个工人;fan-in = 结果从多处收拢回一点。像折扇:从扇轴散开是 out,收拢回来是 in。
用法:可作名词(a fan-out of 3 auditors)也可作动词(to fan out the work)。硬件圈的老词——一个输出接多个负载就叫 fan-out,软件圈借来描述任务分发。
② FAN-OUT / FAN-IN
The afternoon's headline pair. Fan-out = tasks scattering from one point to many workers; fan-in = results converging from many back to one. Like a folding fan: opening from the pivot is out, closing back is in.
Usage: noun ("a fan-out of 3 auditors") or verb ("to fan out the work"). An old hardware term — one output driving many loads was fan-out; software borrowed it for task distribution.
③ short-circuit — 短路
上午 B 卷跑出 NO BUGS FOUND,校验官根本没出场,账单 1179 tokens 全场最低——这条便宜的路叫 short-circuit。
拆一处:电气里"短路"是电流抄近路;代码里 && 左边是 false 时右边直接不算,叫 short-circuit evaluation。同一个词,同一层意思:近路一通,后面全免。
③ SHORT-CIRCUIT
In the morning, paper B returned NO BUGS FOUND, the critic never took the stage, and the bill came to 1179 tokens — the cheapest run of the day. That cheap path is a short-circuit.
One note: in electronics a short circuit is current taking a shortcut; in code, when the left of && is false the right never runs — short-circuit evaluation. Same word, same idea: once the shortcut opens, everything after is skipped.
④ wall-clock — 墙钟时间
程序输出里的 wall-clock=61.9s:你手表真实走过的时间。它和"CPU 时间"相对——三个 agent 等 API 回包时,CPU 几乎在睡觉,但墙上的钟照走。并联优化的就是墙钟:61.9s ≈ 最慢的一段,而不是三段之和。
记法:钟挂在墙上,对所有人都一样公平地走——真实流逝的时间。
④ WALL-CLOCK
From today's output: wall-clock=61.9s — the time your watch actually measured. Contrast with "CPU time": while three agents wait for API replies, the CPU mostly sleeps, but the clock on the wall keeps walking. Parallelism optimizes wall-clock: 61.9s ≈ the slowest segment, not the sum of three.
Memory hook: the clock on the wall ticks the same for everyone — real elapsed time.
⑤ judgeable — 可判定的
段审计员的口头禅 NOT JUDGEABLE FROM SEGMENT:仅凭我这一段,判不了。judge + able——注意拼写是 judgeable,judge 结尾的 e 保留后再加 -able。
这个词今天立了大功:三段共 6 次 NOT JUDGEABLE,零编造。它和 refute 是一对:判不了是诚实,REFUTED 是拿证据驳倒——你的校验官只做后者,不越界做前者。
⑤ JUDGEABLE
The segment auditors' refrain: NOT JUDGEABLE FROM SEGMENT — "from my slice alone, I cannot rule". Judge + able — note the spelling: judgeable, the e survives before -able.
It earned its keep today: six NOT-JUDGEABLE verdicts, zero fabrication. It pairs with refute: "not judgeable" is honesty; REFUTED is overturning WITH evidence — your critic does only the latter, never trespassing into the former.
