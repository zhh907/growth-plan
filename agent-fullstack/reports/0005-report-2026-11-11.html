三、Today's English 今天遇到的英语 (5)

全部来自今天的真实场景,每条讲一个点 · All met in the wild today, one note each
① fabricate — 你的 agent 今天亲口用的词
你敲错文件名的时候,它说:"I will not fabricate findings for a file I cannot read."(我不会为一个读不到的文件编造结论)
fabricate = 编造,比 make up 正式,报错、拒答、正式声明里都常见。顺带看一眼语法:"a file I cannot read" 是个省掉了 that 的定语从句——"一个我读不到的文件"。
① FABRICATE — YOUR AGENT'S OWN WORD TODAY
When you typed the wrong filename, it said: "I will not fabricate findings for a file I cannot read."
"fabricate" = to make up, but formal — common in errors, refusals, official statements. One grammar note: "a file I cannot read" is a relative clause with "that" omitted — "a file that I cannot read".
② refute — 驳倒,要拿证据的那种
明天 critic 的裁决词就这两个:VERIFIED(核实通过)/ REFUTED(被驳倒)。
refute 和 deny 的区别值得记:deny 是嘴上说"不是我不是",refute 是把证据摆桌上"你看,这行代码明明做了防护"。一个是态度,一个是论证。
② REFUTE — TO DISPROVE, WITH EVIDENCE
Tomorrow the critic speaks in exactly two verdicts: VERIFIED / REFUTED.
Worth remembering: "deny" vs "refute". To deny is to say "not me" — an attitude. To refute is to lay the evidence on the table — "look, line 35 clearly normalizes the lots." One is a stance, the other an argument.
③ control group — 这里的 control 不是"控制"
别被字面骗了:control group 里的 control 是"对照、基准"的意思,不是控制谁。就像 controlled experiment 叫"对照实验",不叫"控制实验"。
用法示例:Without a control group, you cannot tell signal from noise.(没有对照组,你分不清信号和噪音。)
③ CONTROL GROUP — "CONTROL" HERE MEANS BASELINE
Do not be fooled by the word: in "control group", control means a baseline for comparison, not commanding anyone. Same as "controlled experiment" — you compare against it.
Example in the wild: "Without a control group, you cannot tell signal from noise."
④ calibration — 给秤定刻度
calibrate 本义是"给仪器标刻度":体重秤用标准砝码调准,温度计用冰水混合物校零。引申出来,calibration 就是"判断标准的一致性"。
今天你的例子就是教材:Same code, opposite verdicts — that is calibration drift.(同样的代码,相反的判决——这就是刻度漂移。)
④ CALIBRATION — SETTING THE SCALE MARKS
"calibrate" originally means marking the scale on an instrument: tuning a scale with standard weights, zeroing a thermometer in ice water. Extended, calibration = the consistency of your judgment.
Today gave you the textbook example: "Same code, opposite verdicts — that is calibration drift."
⑤ severity — 严重度,和 security 是亲戚
severe(严重的)+ ity(名词后缀)= severity。同一家的还有 security(安全)、simplicity(简单)——见到 -ity 结尾,八成是名词。
你的报告里它天天上岗:[SEVERITY HIGH] = 高严重度。分级就是 HIGH / MED / LOW。
⑤ SEVERITY — A COUSIN OF "SECURITY"
severe + the noun suffix -ity = severity. Same family: security, simplicity — see -ity at the end, almost certainly a noun.
It reports for duty in every one of your reports: [SEVERITY HIGH]. The ladder is HIGH / MED / LOW.
